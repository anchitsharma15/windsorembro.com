A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WONDER

A little clarity.
Before we begin.

What can I customise?+

Headwear, sweatshirts, bags and custom artwork are the starting points in this developing collection. Describe your piece, design and preferred placement in the project planner.

Can I shop the collection online?+

The collection is currently in development. The images showcase design inspiration; online ordering and checkout are not available yet.

How do pricing and timelines work?+

These need to be confirmed for each project. Include the type of piece, quantity, artwork and ideal date in your brief so the details are ready to discuss.

Does the planner send an inquiry?+

No. It prepares a brief that you can copy or download. It doesn’t send your details, confirm availability or reserve an order.
